# Product Sense

## Purpose and intended users

Let's Farty is a proposed iPhone/Android app for playful recording and sharing, with optional private food/symptom tracking and evidence-linked digestive guidance. The [MVP design](design/active/2026-10-14-lets-farty-mvp-design.md) owns product requirements and pending choices.

## Product boundaries

Ranked scores are calculated by the server. Ranking opt-in and audio-sharing opt-in are separate. Health entries stay private. Sound scores describe acoustic qualities and must not imply diagnosis or a measure of digestive health.

## Success metrics

No numerical adoption, retention, health, or launch targets have been approved. Current repository success means reproducible tooling and useful, accurate contributor guidance. The foundation and later feature plans must define their concrete completion checks before implementation.
